Crispy Street-Style Bread Pakoda

A popular North Indian snack made with spiced potato stuffing packed between bread slices, dipped in a chickpea flour batter, and deep-fried to golden perfection. Best served hot with mint chutney and masala chai!

Quick Info

Details:

  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 4 pieces

Ingredients

  • 4 white or brown bread slices
  • 2 medium boiled and mashed potatoes
  • 1 cup chickpea flour (besan)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ajwain (carom seeds)
  • A pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water (for making the batter)
  • Oil (for deep frying)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare the potato filling: In a bowl, mix the mashed potatoes with salt, red chili powder, and a pinch of turmeric. Set aside.
  2. Make the batter: In a separate mixing bowl, combine the chickpea flour, ajwain, turmeric powder, red chili powder, hing, and salt. Gradually add water to make a smooth, medium-thick batter.
  3. Assemble the sandwiches: Take two bread slices. Spread the spiced potato mixture evenly on one slice, then cover it with the second slice. Cut the sandwich diagonally into two triangles.
  4. Heat the oil: Heat oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Coat and fry: Dip each bread triangle into the chickpea batter, making sure it is fully coated. Gently drop it into the hot oil.
  6. Fry until golden: Fry on medium heat, turning occasionally, until the pakoda turns crispy and golden-brown. Remove and drain excess oil on paper towels.
  7. Serve: Enjoy hot with green chutney and ketchup!
