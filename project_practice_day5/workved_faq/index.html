FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

This is a simple FAQ page for the Workved.com website.

Is Workved free to use?

Yes. Generating your layout and viewing your full Bill of Quantities is free. You only sign in when you want to download the PDF or submit it for review.

What exactly is a Bill of Quantities?

An itemized list of every piece of furniture, fixture and material needed to fit out your office — with quantities and costs — that you can hand straight to a contractor.

How accurate is the estimate?

Estimates use industry-standard space-planning norms and a curated, priced furniture catalog. You can fine-tune every space type before finalizing.

Can I customize the space mix?

After setting your area and team size, the layout builder lets you adjust workstations, cabins, meeting rooms and common areas to your needs.

Do I still need an interior designer?

Workved gives you a professional-grade BOQ on its own — though you're free to share it with any designer or contractor you like.

Which locations do you support?

All major Indian metros, with pricing and availability tuned to your city.
