Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India and the People's President

Potrait


1931 – 2015 | Scientist, Statesman, and Educator

About His Life

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a humble background, he worked hard to support his family while pursuing his education. He went on to study physics and aerospace engineering, eventually playing a pivotal role in India's civilian space program and military missile development.

Major Milestones

  • Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007, affectionately known as the "People's President."
  • Played a crucial leadership role as a scientist at DRDO and ISRO, earning the title "Missile Man of India" for his work on the ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology.
  • Led the successful Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998 as the Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister.
  • Authored inspiring books such as "Wings of Fire" and "Ignited Minds" to motivate the youth of India.
  • Received India's highest civilian honor, the Bharat Ratna, in 1997.

Inspiring Words

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."
